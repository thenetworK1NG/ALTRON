03  /  capabilities

Not just chat.
A helper that acts.

Other bots only talk. This one also does — it runs things, moves files, and grows into whatever kind of helper you need it to be.

A helper that actually acts

Ask in normal language and ALTRON does the work on your computer — then brings the result back to your WhatsApp. No buttons to hunt for and no technical vocabulary.

ask · approve · done

01

Your chats, remembered

Ask what you and someone discussed today, find an old detail, or request a full chat export.

02

Messages with a safety check

Tell ALTRON what to say. It shows you the exact person and draft first, then sends only after you reply YES.

03

Webcam when you ask

Request a webcam picture or a short video clip. Clips are capped at 60 seconds and delivered straight to your private chat.

04

Built-in sentry mode

Arm it when you leave. Local motion detection can send you a camera alert, with a cooldown so your phone does not get spammed.

05

It keeps itself going

Internet drops, restarts, and session problems are handled automatically where possible. You only get asked for a QR scan when WhatsApp truly needs one.

06

Private by design

It runs on your computer. Camera controls and chat intelligence stay owner-only, with explicit confirmation for actions that contact other people.

07

Text, painted

"Send me an image of a fox in anime style" — pick from photorealistic, 3d render, watercolor, pixel art and more. Add an image-generation key once and pictures come back watermark-free.

08

YouTube on tap

"Play me lo-fi beats" streams audio straight through your speakers. Queue whole lists at once, then pause, skip or seek from your phone — music your computer plays in one message.

09

Alarms that ring

"Set an alarm for 07:30, wake-up call" — your PC rings a siren and pops a full-screen overlay with snooze, so nothing important slips past. Set, list and cancel them from a text.

10

Control from a text

Turn the PC's sound up or down, mute it, request a shutdown that waits for your YES, or stop the current task instantly with "/bot stop". Your machine, on your say-so.

It becomes whatever you need

Today it answers your messages. Tomorrow it's your assistant, your errand-runner, your night-shift worker. Anything your computer can do, you can teach it to do from a text. You decide what it becomes next.

07  /  connect

Getting connected is a scan, not a setup.

No installers to wrestle with, no lines of code to paste. The whole thing connects with QR codes — the same easy way you link WhatsApp on a new phone. Two scans, and you're talking to your computer.

scan 1

Link WhatsApp

Open WhatsApp, go to Linked Devices, and scan the code with your phone. One scan and your bot is in your pocket — answering your texts from anywhere.

like linking a new phone · done in seconds

scan 2

Link GitHub

The same easy trick. Approve the login and your bot can push code, create projects and manage your repositories — straight from a text message.

one approve · your repos, from your phone

That's the whole setup. No cables, no code, no account juggling. The one-click installer walks you through all of it — requirements, an optional image-generation key, GitHub, then the QR link. Two minutes, and you're live.
